I will begin with world enablers because they're more interesting in design.

World enablers refer to anything that supports out of combat. World enablers can be split into crafting enablers and party enablers.

Any and every class can also spec into world enablers.

However, leveling up every enabler class is difficult. Your max crafting enabler level is dependent on every enabling class. Additionally, leveling up a specific class takes skill points from your total crafting enabler level, which doesn't apply to all crafting enabler classes. Luckily, the higher level your other enabling classes, the more proficient your designs involving those parts are and the higher xp you get for your current classes.

Crafting enablers are focused on crafting, whether that's forging armor, tanning leather, or enchanting, crafting enablers are what drive the economy.

there are many types of crafting enablers like: [Blacksmiths](Blacksmithing.md), [Leather Workers](leatherworking.md), [Gem Refiners](gemworking.md), 

Party enablers work on supporting a number of party members via their skills.

There are also many party enablers, which includes skills like: [foragers](Foraging), chefs, haulers, merchants, miners(I LOVE MINERS), lumberjacks, and builders.
