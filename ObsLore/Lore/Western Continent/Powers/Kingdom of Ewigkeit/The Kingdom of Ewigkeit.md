The kingdom is the central power of the [[Etulian High Plains]]. While their wealth and culture is rich, they have adopted an isolationist policy, rejecting external travelers. Additionally, they possess many internal problems.

Conflicts:
Internal banditry funded by external bandit municiplalities.
Conflict with Mage Tower over influence over their internal mage association.
Political corruption manifesting in form of split house of nobles.

This is the starting zone and will be the primary place for the first 15 ish levels.
