The kingdom is the central power of the [[Etulian High Plains]]. While their wealth and culture is rich, they have adopted an isolationist policy, rejecting external travelers. Additionally, they possess many internal problems.

Conflicts:
Internal banditry funded by external bandit towns.
Conflict with Mage Tower over influence over their internal mage association.
Political corruption manifesting in form of split house of nobles.

This is the starting zone and will be the primary place for the first 15 ish levels.

key cities:

[[Bastion]]
[[Preisk]]

The citizens are called Ewigites. Think a blend of your traditional fantasy land and renaissance Germany.



Sections:
The kingdom is split into multiple sections: Al, Ba, Cul, De, and Et.

Each section is ruled by a minor lord, who is an elected official who originates from the area with backing from the King.

[[Al]] is the first sector, ruled by the major lord, who is chosen directly by the King. It contains [[Cadarnle]], the capital, and is where the Royal family, the magic tower, and the Barrier. It is located at the edge, where the 

[[Ba]] is the second, and contains Bastion. Ba is the group that overlooks the gate, carefully deciding who and what can enter through the valley. Ba is primarily in charge of mining operations and the management of the gate.

[[Cul]] is the third section. It is the primary producer of food and is the center of cattle and grain. It's nestled within a series of crater valleys.

[[De]] is the hard craft center of the kingdom. Populated by a small population of humans who inherited a dwarven legacy, When it comes to forging bits, weapons, and daily tools, nothing beats out De in the kingdom. It's also filled with "mercenary" and adventure guilds.

[[Et]] is the fifth, now deprecated section. While it is still ruled by a lord, ever since the isolation, Et has been destitute. Et made its living through managing external trade and communication.





The food tends to consist of sheep and goat cheeses(dairy), grains(barley, rye), and mutton.

Housing is made of stone most frequently.

Wood is status symbol due to its rarity(very little present and no further imports. Wood embalmers are a dying brand but still highly prestigious craft profession.)

The people are shorter and slightly more compact.

There is no centralized religion, so each section has its own variations of the [[Old Religion]].
