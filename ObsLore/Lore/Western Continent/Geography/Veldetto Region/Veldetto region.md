The veldetto region is a large crater shaped area primarily composed of plains. It was the nest for the Nero(nare - ough) culture, which later formed powers like the kingdom of Ewigkeit.

The Old religion was the predominant belief of the people in this area due to the strong elemental roots of the area.

The Veldetto region will be the primary 0-30 area, and serves as a vast, multi biome region.
