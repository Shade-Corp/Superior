The Old Religion is a modern name referring to Ter'naquitism, a religion which worshipped elemental deities. Adopting a Romanesque policy of merging pantheons, Ter'naquitism was the formal "peasants" religion of the veldetto region.

Mythos:
Once, before there was any, a spirit died along the "chaos", vel.  From its death, all was born. The world rose

The hair became the flowing rivers.
The skin burnt and crackled and wrapped the earth.
the mouth formed gaping cracks in the earth,
The blood streamed and became men
The breath became Mana.

From the ground, the sky, and the ether(inbetween), the beings of nature, Known as the ter, came to be. The plains of Veldetto are themselves, the temple of the spirit. 

The mythos primarily follows a central "family" of Ter.

Consisting of 
"the mother": Modron
"The Elder": Andrhynn
"The younger": Annwynn
"The father": Blerwed
"The world": Veldetto

Modron represents the matriarch of the group. She raises her young through harsh lessons. Kind, yet strict, Modron determines both seasons, and fertility. 

The elder is the patient Ter of water. The son of Modrom and Blerwed, he was entitled to recieve everything, before being dashed to pieces by his brother. His tears are often accompanied by his brother's rage. When he dies, his blood freezes over and his mother mourns.

The younger is the rageful Ter of storms and is envious of his older brother.

The father is an ambivalent rarely mentioned member who connects all things together. He holds up the sky, whilst also being an embodiment of life.

The world is the forgotten fifth Ter, and is the one who makes things real. God of entryways, time, and relation. Without Veldetto, there is no effect for cause.

As the mythos goes,
From their birth thousands of years ago, they roamed the earth, having multiple interactions with other deities.


The primary story follows:
Annwynn's various attempts to steal his brother's rightful position. After a number of attempts to sabotage him, resulting in rain,
Annwynn decieves Andrhynn and convinces him to meet him high above his father's domain.
